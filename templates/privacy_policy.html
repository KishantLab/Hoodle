Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 | Hoodle Learning Management System

1. Overview

Hoodle LMS ("we", "our", or "the Application") is an educational classroom management platform developed for academic institutions. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile and web applications collect, process, and safeguard student and faculty information.

2. Device Permissions & Usage

  • Camera Access (CAMERA): Used solely for scanning time-rotating classroom QR codes on projector screens to verify student attendance. The camera stream is processed locally in real-time; no photos or video recordings are saved or transmitted to external servers.
  • Location Information (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION): Used exclusively during attendance check-in to confirm physical presence within instructor-authorized classroom boundaries (geofencing). Location coordinates are evaluated strictly during attendance submission and are never tracked continuously in the background.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to deliver immediate alerts for published announcements, grade releases, classwork deadlines, and direct academic messages from instructors.
  • Storage & Files: Used strictly when students or instructors voluntarily upload or download assignment submissions, PDF reports, or source code files.

3. Data Security & Retention

All communications between the Hoodle client applications and server clusters are encrypted using TLS/HTTPS. Student educational records, assignments, and grades are protected under institutional guidelines and are never sold or shared with commercial third parties.
