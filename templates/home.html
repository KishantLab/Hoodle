Quick Onboarding

Getting Started in 4 Simple Steps

Everything you need to join classes and submit lab assignments in under two minutes.

1

Sign In or Register

Use your IIT Bhilai roll number (e.g. B26CS021) to sign in, or click Register to create your student account.

2

Join Your Classroom

Use your professor's 6-character class code or direct short link to enroll in your courses with a single click.

3

Install Android App

Download the official APK to scan the rotating dynamic QR code in lecture halls and mark attendance without delay.

4

Submit & Excel

Submit lab code archives before the countdown timer expires and get your cryptographic receipt instantly.
